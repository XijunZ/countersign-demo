UP 24.6% IN A SINGLE YEAR
The Larkspire UK Equity Income Fund returned 24.6% in the 12 months to 31 March 2022 - our strongest year since launch and well ahead of the wider UK market. It's a result that shows what disciplined stock-picking can achieve.

A guaranteed income you can plan around
For many of our investors, what matters most is a reliable income. The fund pays out quarterly and currently yields 5.1% a year, giving you a guaranteed income stream to plan your retirement around. Because we focus on large, established, dividend-paying companies, your capital is protected from the worst of market swings while you enjoy the income.

Why leave it in the bank?
The average easy-access savings account pays around 1.9% today. Over the same period our fund's yield is more than two and a half times that - so every pound left in cash is a pound that could be working harder for you. Moving even part of your savings could make a real difference to your income in retirement.

What our managers are watching
Co-managers Helen Marsh-Carter and Tom Adeyemi continue to favour financially strong companies with well-covered dividends, particularly in consumer staples, utilities and selected banks. They have trimmed exposure to housebuilders, where they see earnings risk if mortgage rates stay higher for longer, and added to two mid-cap industrials trading at attractive valuations. The portfolio holds 48 stocks, with the top ten making up 37% of the fund.
