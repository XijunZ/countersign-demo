Here is our quarterly update on the Wexmoor Sterling Strategic Bond Fund, which you hold through your Wexmoor account.

What the fund aims to do
The fund aims to provide an income, with the potential for some capital growth, over any five-year period. It invests mainly in sterling-denominated bonds issued by governments and companies, including some high-yield (sub-investment-grade) bonds, which pay more interest because the issuer is more likely to default. There is no guarantee the fund will meet its objective over any period.

The fund fell sharply in the year to September 2022 as interest rates rose quickly, which pushed bond prices down. It has recovered since, but it underperformed its comparator in the year to September 2025. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Income
The fund's current distribution yield is 5.4% and its underlying yield is 4.9% (I Income share class, as at 30 September 2026). Yields are not guaranteed and will change as the portfolio and market conditions change. The distribution yield is higher than the underlying yield because the fund's charges are taken from capital, which increases the income paid out but reduces the potential for capital growth.

The main risks
- Interest rate risk: when interest rates rise, bond prices usually fall.
- Credit risk: a bond issuer may fail to pay interest or repay capital. About 28% of the fund is in high-yield bonds, where this risk is greater.
- Liquidity risk: in stressed markets some bonds can be hard to sell at a fair price.
- Derivatives: the fund uses derivatives to manage risk, which can magnify losses as well as gains.
A full list of risks is in the Key Investor Information Document (KIID) and Prospectus.
